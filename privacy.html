Privacy Policy

Vault · Last updated 7 November 2026

Vault is a number puzzle game made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game has no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and your games stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Information stored on your device

The players you add, their finished games (codes, guesses and times) and your settings are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. Clearing the game's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them.

The optional leaderboard

Nothing is sent anywhere unless a player chooses to join the leaderboard. For a player who joins, the game sends, for each code length they've played (3, 4 or 5 digits):

  • their player name. Please don't use your real name;
  • the number of games played and cracked, the total number of guesses in cracked games, and the fewest guesses in one game;
  • a random ID created on your device, so the entry can be updated. It isn't linked to you, your device or any account.

Names and stats are shown publicly on the leaderboard. The random ID is never shown. This information is stored by our database provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. We use it only to run the leaderboard, and our legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time: tap Remove on the leaderboard screen to delete the entry straight away. Deleting a player from the game also removes them.

Names are checked by a word filter. Players can report a name they find inappropriate; a name reported by several players is hidden, and we may hide or remove names that break these rules. A report stores the reported name and a random ID for the reporter's device, so each device can only report a name once.

Playing a friend

In a room, your player name and your guesses (and, at the end of the game, the code you set) are shared live with the other player in the same room, through our provider's real-time service. Rooms aren't stored: once both players leave, nothing about the room is kept. Rooms can only be joined with their code or invite link.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, the servers that deliver the game (GitHub Pages) and the leaderboard and rooms (Supabase) may briefly record technical information such as your IP address and browser type in their logs, to keep their services running and secure. We don't use this information and don't receive it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The leaderboard is optional and asks players not to use real names.

How long we keep information

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs, and are deleted automatically after 12 months without activity. You can delete an entry at any time with Remove, or by asking us.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete information about you, or object to how it's used. To make a request, email the developer at the contact address shown on the game's store listing. If you joined the leaderboard, include the name you used. We will reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
