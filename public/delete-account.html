Delete your Vault account

Vault: Crack the Code · by Sterls

You can delete your Vault account and all its data at any time. This works the same in the Android app and in the browser.

In the game (straight away)

  1. Open Vault and tap Settings on the home screen.
  2. Tap your name at the top (Account).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and the game starts again with a new, empty guest account.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game, email the developer at the contact address shown on Vault's Google Play listing, with the subject "Delete my Vault account". Include the player name you used and, if you signed in with Google, that Google account's email address, so we can find your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including its link to your Google account (and the Google email address) if you signed in with Google.
  • Your player name and leaderboard entries.
  • All your games: daily codes, single player and room games, with their guesses, times and results.
  • Your room memberships, and any reports you made about other players' names.
  • On the device you delete from: your saved games, stats and settings.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Supabase) may keep technical server logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time for security; we don't use them. See the privacy policy for details.
