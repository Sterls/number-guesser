Privacy Policy

Vault · Last updated 7 November 2026 (accounts and server-checked games)

Vault is a number puzzle game made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game has no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and your account holds only what the game needs.

Your account

The first time you open the game it creates a guest account for your phone with our database provider, Supabase. It has a random ID and no personal details. If you choose Sign in with Google, the account is linked to your Google account, and Supabase receives your Google account's email address and ID so you can sign in again on any phone. We don't receive your Google password and don't use your email for anything else.

Your games

To keep games fair, ranked games (the daily code, single player when you're online, and games against friends) are played through our server: it makes the secret code, checks each guess and stores the game (the guesses, the times and the result) with your account. Your name is stored with your account too. This is how your stats and streaks work, and why they can follow you to a new phone.

Single player games played offline, your settings and a copy of your stats are kept in your browser's storage on your own device.

The leaderboard (optional)

You only appear on the leaderboard if you choose to join. It shows your name and your results (guesses, times, games played and daily streak) publicly. Please don't use your real name. You can hide yourself at any time from the leaderboard or the Account screen.

Names are checked by a word filter. Players can report a name they find inappropriate; a name reported by several players is hidden, and we may hide or remove names that break these rules. A report stores the reported name and the reporter's account ID, so each player can only report a name once.

Playing friends

In a room, the other players see your name and how many guesses you've made; when a round ends they also see your guesses, your time and the code. Rooms can only be joined with their code or invite link, and are deleted after a week without use.

Who processes it

Accounts, games and the leaderboard are stored by Supabase, which processes them on our behalf. Google sign-in is provided by Google. We use this information only to run the game. Our legal basis is providing the service you asked for (your account and games) and your consent (the leaderboard and Google sign-in), which you can withdraw at any time.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, the servers that deliver the game (GitHub Pages) and the leaderboard and rooms (Supabase) may briefly record technical information such as your IP address and browser type in their logs, to keep their services running and secure. We don't use this information and don't receive it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The leaderboard is optional and asks players not to use real names.

How long we keep information

Your account and games are kept while you use the game. You can delete your account and everything with it at any time with Delete my account (Settings → Account), or by asking us: see how to delete your account. Rooms are deleted after a week without use.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete information about you, or object to how it's used. To make a request, email the developer at the contact address shown on the game's store listing. If you joined the leaderboard, include the name you used. We will reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
